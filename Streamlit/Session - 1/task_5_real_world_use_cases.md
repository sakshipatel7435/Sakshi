Task 5: Streamlit Use Cases for Popular Apps

Here are three practical scenarios where Streamlit could be utilized in major apps:
1. Zomato (Restaurant Performance & Demand Dashboard):Streamlit allows internal analytics teams to build interactive dashboards for regional managers to track real-time delivery performance, popular food trends, and restaurant ratings across cities. 

2. Flipkart (Inventory & Pricing Optimization Tool): Streamlit enables supply chain teams to quickly test and internalize machine learning models that forecast demand spikes and adjust warehouse stock levels dynamically during sales events.

3. Spotify (Personalized Recommendation Model Tester): Streamlit lets data scientists visually test and tweak audio-feature algorithms (like genre clustering or playlist generation models) with live sliders before deploying them into the core production app.
